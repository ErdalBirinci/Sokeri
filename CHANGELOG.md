# Changelog

## 1.1.0

- Predicted low/high alerts with a dashed projection drawn on the chart
- Signal-loss watch: warns when no new reading arrives for 20 minutes
- Snooze buttons (15 min / 1 h) on threshold and prediction notifications
- Configurable alert repeat (once / 5 / 10 / 15 / 30 min); night lows always
  escalate to every 5 minutes with a louder sound
- Distinct sounds: normal, urgent night low, signal loss, prediction, test
- Reports view: 7-day time-in-range bar, weekly daily-pattern heatmap, CSV
  export, one-page PDF report for doctor visits, full-tab clinic mode
- Dexcom edition: multiple signed-in accounts with an in-settings switcher
- Landing page: live simulated-day chart, delta, below/above stats,
  troubleshooting FAQ, open-source + donation sections

## 1.0.0

- Initial release: Libre and Dexcom editions
- Toolbar badge value, trend arrow, 3/6/12/24-hour and 7-day charts
- TIR, average, GMI, %CV, lowest/highest statistics
- High/low threshold alerts with separate night thresholds
- Event markers, CSV export, mg/dL ↔ mmol/L, light/dark themes, English UI
